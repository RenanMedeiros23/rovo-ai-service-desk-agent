# KB-001 — SAP System Unavailable

## Category

Enterprise Applications

## Affected Service

SAP ERP

## Symptoms

- Users cannot access SAP.
- Connection errors are displayed.
- Business transactions cannot be completed.
- Production activities may be interrupted.

## Initial Troubleshooting

1. Confirm the reported error message.
2. Identify the number of affected users.
3. Determine when the issue started.
4. Verify network connectivity.
5. Check available service status information.
6. Search for related incidents.
7. Verify whether an approved workaround exists.

## Escalation Criteria

- Multiple users are affected.
- Critical business operations are interrupted.
- Initial troubleshooting is unsuccessful.
- Specialized SAP support is required.

## Recommended Support Team

SAP Application Support — Level 2.

## Service Restoration Validation

- Confirm that users can access SAP.
- Verify that business transactions can be completed.
- Request confirmation from affected users.
- Document the resolution or workaround.

## Safety and Restrictions

- Do not restart production servers without authorization.
- Do not modify SAP configurations without approval.
- Do not assume or communicate an unverified root cause.
